Our Story

From R500 to
a legacy

👩‍🍳
Founder
Dimakatso
Hlongwane
Mama Dee · Danville, Pretoria

What started with R500 and a single batch of amagwinya has grown into one of Danville's most beloved fast food spots. Dimakatso built Mama Dee's from the ground up, expanding her menu based on what her community craved, creating 6 jobs along the way.

"My customers love and prefer amagwinya made by me. They can somehow tell if I didn't make them."
